# Advanced Fourier and Wavelet Transforms in AI Engineering

Mathematical foundations of continuous and discrete Fourier Transforms (FT, DFT, FFT) and Wavelet Transforms for signal, time-series, and spatial representation in deep neural networks.
Fourier decomposition expresses signals as linear combinations of orthogonal basis sinusoids:
$$X[k] = \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} x[n] e^{-i 2\pi k n / N}$$
Wavelet transforms overcome the uncertainty principle trade-off via multi-resolution time-frequency localization.
