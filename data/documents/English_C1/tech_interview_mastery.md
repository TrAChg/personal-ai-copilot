# High-Impact English Idioms and Phrases for FAANG Tech Interviews

## Overview
Essential natural English idioms, transitions, and phrasing patterns for senior AI/Systems engineering interviews.

## 1. Navigating Trade-Offs & Complexity
- **"It's a double-edged sword"**: When discussing solutions with significant pros and cons (e.g., in-memory caching vs. cache invalidation complexity).
- **"At first glance..., but upon closer inspection..."**: Demonstrates analytical depth rather than jumping to conclusions.
- **"Strike the right balance"**: Used when optimizing between latency, throughput, and consistency.

## 2. Distributed Systems & Architectural Discussion
- **"Graceful degradation"**: Ensuring that under high load, non-critical services fail without crashing the core system.
- **"Decouple state from computation"**: Clean design pattern for scalable distributed microservices and stream processing.
- **"Blast radius mitigation"**: Limiting the failure impact through circuit breakers and partition boundaries.

## 3. Natural Connectors for System Design
- *"To walk you through my thought process..."*
- *"Let's establish our baseline constraints before diving into the component design."*
- *"We can benchmark this against a naive approach to measure real-world performance gains."*
